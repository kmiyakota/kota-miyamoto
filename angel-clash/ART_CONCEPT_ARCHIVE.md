## Current target style (new decision)

On 2026-11-05, the creator requested a **less computationally intensive game**: more subdued backgrounds and effects, and all three protagonists wearing **cleaner fitted miniskirt battle costumes with minimal frills and limited accessories**. The underlying person canon remains the creator's Wix [３人について](https://miyakota3.wixsite.com/my-site-1/post/about-three-new): ITO's warm-brown long hair, AKARI's short dark-brown bob, TAMAKI's dark short bob + glasses and feminine identity, etc. NOX retains the approved dark-angel concept. Do not introduce new faces or identities merely to simplify clothing.

The previous frilled outfits above are **preserved alternatives**, not discarded mistakes. Treat this as outfit-version management, not character replacement.

## Historical decisions

- Original trio character canon: Wix post and original profile boards.
- Earlier ornate battle costumes: approved for the game on 2026-11-05, then explicitly requested to be **kept** as alternative concepts.
- Later main-design direction: fitted miniskirt silhouettes, minimal ruffles, lighter stage and VFX.
- Existing combat atlases and the earlier concept images must remain preserved until verified replacement artwork and multi-frame animation are prepared.
